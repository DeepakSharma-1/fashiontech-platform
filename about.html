01 / About ALOKIT

A place for
what's next.

ALOKIT is being built for people who have ideas for clothing but need a place to develop, share and potentially produce them. We want to make the first step into fashion more open.

Why we started

Great ideas don't wait for an invitation. Neither should the people behind them.

Many emerging designers have imagination and a point of view, but may not have access to design tools, industry networks, manufacturers or a platform to show their work. ALOKIT aims to bring those first steps together in one place.

Designers can create independently, use AI suggestions only if they choose, publish work under their own identity and apply to have selected designs considered for limited-edition production.

02 / Meet the founder

Built from a simple belief.

I'm Deepak Sharma, the founder of ALOKIT. I started with a question: what if someone with a fresh clothing idea could create it, put their name on it and have a genuine chance to see it become a real product?

I want ALOKIT to make that first step easier for emerging designers. You should not need to already be established to share your creativity. The platform is being built to give new voices a place to experiment, learn, get discovered and grow.

We're at the beginning of this journey. My goal is to build it with designers, listen to the community and create opportunities responsibly.

03 / Our principles

Creative freedom
comes first.

01

Your design stays yours.

Submitting a concept for review does not itself transfer ownership. Any production licence or commercial agreement must be discussed separately.

02

AI is optional.

AI can offer suggestions, but designers decide whether to use them. Originality and personal expression lead the process.

03

Clear opportunities.

Selection, production quantities, credit and compensation should be explained and agreed before manufacturing begins.

01 / The ALOKIT process

From first sketch
to first release.

Here's the proposed journey for designers using ALOKIT. We want every stage to be clear, optional where possible and based on the designer's consent.

01 — Create

Choose your garment.

Open the studio, choose a T-shirt, hoodie, jacket, jeans, shorts or another garment, then customise it with colour, artwork and your own creative details.

02 — Explore

Use AI if you want.

AI suggestions are optional. You can ask for help exploring colours, patterns or silhouettes, or complete the design entirely yourself.

03 — Submit

Tell us about your design.

Provide your name, contact details, design title, signature, requested quantity and consent for ALOKIT to review the design for possible manufacturing.

04 — Review

We review the idea.

ALOKIT may review originality, feasibility, customer interest, sample costs and manufacturing availability. Submission does not guarantee selection.

05 / Manufacturing and limited editions

Made with permission.
Credited to its creator.

Designers can request a production quantity starting at 10 pieces. The requested quantity is a preference, not a confirmed order. Before any production, ALOKIT and the designer must agree in writing on final quantity, sample approval, costs, selling price, designer compensation or royalty, credit, timeline and the rights granted for manufacturing.

ALOKIT will not treat the submission form as a transfer of ownership or as final manufacturing approval. The designer keeps ownership unless a separate written agreement says otherwise.

06 / Before you submit

Know what
you're agreeing to.

Original work

Submit work you can share.

Confirm that you created the design or have permission to submit all included artwork, logos and images.

Quantity

Ten pieces minimum.

The form requires at least 10 requested pieces. ALOKIT will discuss feasibility and confirm any final quantity separately.

Consent

Review is not production.

You can allow ALOKIT to review your design for possible production. Manufacturing starts only after a separate written agreement.
